01 — OUR STORY

Carrying on a father’s legacy,
one bolt at a time.

In 1999, Mr. Tai walked away from a stable career at Cycle & Carriage to do something that mattered more to him: carry on his father’s legacy as a mechanic. What started as a small workshop built on years of hands-on experience has grown into MBS, a trusted name for car owners in Section 51A, Petaling Jaya and beyond, known especially for its expertise in German makes like BMW, Mercedes-Benz, and Volkswagen, alongside reliable Japanese and Malaysian brands such as Toyota, Honda, and Proton.

Mr. Tai’s passion for cars runs deep, and it shows just as much in how he treats his own vehicles as it does in how he treats yours. Every nut, bolt, and wire gets the same meticulous attention, because to him, there’s no such thing as a small job or someone else’s car, just cars that deserve to be taken care of properly.

At the heart of MBS is a simple philosophy: treat every customer like family.

Whether you’re coming in for a routine oil change or a thorough pre purchase inspection before buying a used car, Mr. Tai takes the time to walk you through exactly what’s going on under the hood, no jargon, no upselling, just honest, detailed advice you can actually understand and trust.

It’s this personal touch, paired with genuine technical expertise and an eye for detail that never switches off, that keeps customers coming back year after year, and why so many say it feels less like a repair shop and more like a friend looking out for you and your car.
